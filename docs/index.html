Dracula knowledge graph

Who shows up with whom in Bram Stoker's novel. Each journal entry, letter, diary page and newspaper cutting is one document. Names are detected with spaCy and grouped into people and places. Two entities are linked when they appear in the same document; thicker lines mean more shared documents.

Built automatically: what to keep in mind
  • Mentions count names and titles ("the Count", "the Professor"). Pronouns are not counted.
  • A bare "Harker" is counted as Jonathan. In the novel Mina is always "Mrs. Harker", "Madam Mina" or "Mina".
  • Types of the less frequent names were checked by hand against the text, down to two mentions. Names mentioned once are not shown.
  • The score is measured against a hand-made answer key that was extended while fixing these errors, so it is not an independent test.
  • Links mean "appear in the same document". Stated relationships (married, engaged, proposed to, patient of, preys on and others) are listed in a node's details with the passage of the novel that states them. Each passage was picked by hand and the build checks it is in the text.
  • Documents follow the book's own section headings, so a letter inside a journal is its own document with its own writer. The source file mislabelled many of these; the narrators shown were checked against the text.
